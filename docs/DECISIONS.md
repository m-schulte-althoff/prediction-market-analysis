# Research Decisions

## 2026-09-11

- Recenter the paper on platform-constituted claims and representational non-equivalence. Retain the determinacy–volume association as a secondary exploratory consequence.
- Define determinacy within contracts and divergence between contracts. Do not interpret one as a proxy for the other.
- Require a plausible witness state and implied settlement on each side before calling a pair truth-condition divergent. Treat automated divergence components as matching diagnostics until validated human coding exists.
- Archive exact official rule text for the election, Federal Reserve, Ukraine minerals, and Zelenskyy-suit cases; keep the leader-departure case linked to the systematic matched sample.
- Correct the Polymarket sample by excluding sports before the volume cap. Correct mixed timestamp parsing and measure cumulative-volume exposure only through the observed close/resolution/retrieval boundary.
- Report within-family estimates explicitly. A pooled or family-aggregated association is a between-family result and cannot establish an ambiguity-driven trading mechanism.
- Do not use the three short, same-event aligned histories as evidence that semantic divergence causes price disagreement.
