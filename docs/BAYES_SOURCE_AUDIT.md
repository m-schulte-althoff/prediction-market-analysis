The study's added step is **our own IS extension**: platforms specify `r_c` or `Y_c`, and a trader's common posterior is applied to each specified payoff mapping. Expected-payout identities and logical probability restrictions are ordinary probability consequences, not new theorems attributed to Meehan and Zhang. The source does not itself develop the prediction-market claim-constitution argument.

Their opaque learning situations concern what is learned, including whether the learner knows what she learns. They are **not** our semantic indeterminacy of a settlement category. A resolution architecture is not an updating rule. Contract differences do not establish a violation of global evidential constancy, and no platform is assigned a Bayesian, meta-conditionalization, dogmatic or truth-rule label.

The empirical transfer remains bounded: archived witnesses establish representation mechanisms; profiles describe sampled portfolios; activity associations are exploratory. Actual common trader beliefs, understanding of rules, realized divergence-driven price effects and causal effects on volume/accuracy are unmeasured. Relation audits inspect all archived clauses; the optional diagnostic additionally requires independently validated fresh synchronized observations and comparable pricing conditions.
